Our Foundation

Who We Are

Who We Are

The Botswana Defence Force protects national sovereignty and ensures peace, stability, and security through disciplined military service to the nation and its people.

Our Mandate

We defend Botswana, support civil authorities, and participate in regional and international peacekeeping operations to maintain peace and stability.

National Service

We respond to emergencies, support disaster relief efforts, and safeguard borders to protect citizens and Botswana's national interests at all times.

Training Excellence

Our soldiers undergo rigorous training focused on discipline, leadership, endurance, and operational readiness for diverse mission environments.

Peacekeeping Role

The BDF contributes to global peacekeeping missions, helping stabilise regions affected by conflict and upholding Botswana's international commitments.

Modernisation

We continuously upgrade our technology, equipment, and strategies to meet modern defence standards and address evolving security challenges.

Community Support

We assist communities through outreach programmes, infrastructure support, and humanitarian assistance during times of need.

Core Values

Discipline, loyalty, integrity, and excellence guide every member of the Botswana Defence Force in every mission and every interaction.
