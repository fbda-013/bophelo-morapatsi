 Structure

Divisions of the BDF

Structured for national security, operational excellence, and strategic readiness across all domains.

Division I

Ground Forces

Responsible for land-based military operations, safeguarding national borders, and maintaining internal security through disciplined and strategic deployment. The backbone of Botswana's territorial defence.

Border Patrol

24/7 border surveillance and control

Infantry Units

Highly trained combat battalions

Division II

Air Wing

Provides air mobility, reconnaissance, and rapid response operations to support both national defence and emergency missions. Delivers advanced aerial surveillance, transport, and combat support capabilities.

Surveillance

Aerial reconnaissance operations

Air Transport

Rapid troop and supply movement

Division III

Special Forces

An elite unit trained for specialized operations including counter-terrorism, reconnaissance, and high-priority mission execution. Selected from top performers across all BDF divisions.

Counter-Terror

Rapid threat neutralisation

Intelligence

Covert reconnaissance missions

Division IV

Engineering Corps

Handles construction, maintenance, and technical support, ensuring operational readiness through engineering excellence and innovation. Essential for sustainable military infrastructure development.

Construction

Military infrastructure projects

Maintenance

Equipment and facility upkeep
