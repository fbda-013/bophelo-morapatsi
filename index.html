Our Structure

Defence Force Divisions

Each division plays a critical role in maintaining peace, defending sovereignty, and ensuring rapid, effective military response.

Ground Forces

Territorial Integrity & Border Security

Responsible for land-based military operations, safeguarding national borders, and maintaining internal security through disciplined strategic deployment.

Air Wing

Aerial Surveillance, Transport & Combat

Provides air mobility, reconnaissance, and rapid response operations to support national defence and emergency missions across Botswana.

Special Forces

Elite Tactical & High-Risk Operations

An elite unit trained for counter-terrorism, reconnaissance, and high-priority mission execution requiring exceptional skill and precision.

Engineering Corps

Infrastructure & Technical Support

Handles construction, maintenance, and technical support, ensuring operational readiness through engineering excellence and innovation nationwide.
